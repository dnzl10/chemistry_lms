Stoichiometry & Balancing Theory

1. The Law of Conservation of Mass

Matter can neither be created nor destroyed in a chemical reaction. Therefore, the number of atoms of each element entering as reactants must exactly equal the number of atoms emerging in the products:

Σ (Atoms of Element E)_reactants = Σ (Atoms of Element E)_products

2. Systematic Balancing Heuristics

  • Step 1: Balance elements that appear in only one reactant and one product first (often metals and carbon).
  • Step 2: Balance Hydrogen next.
  • Step 3: Balance Oxygen last, as it frequently appears across multiple compounds and elemental O₂.
  • Step 4 (Odd-Even Rule): If an element has an odd count on one side and an even count on the other, multiply by 2 to clear fractional coefficients.

3. Simplification

Standard chemical convention requires coefficients to be expressed in the lowest whole-number integer ratio. If all coefficients share a common divisor, divide through completely.
