Act as a Senior UI/UX Designer and Lead Frontend Auditor. Your objective is to conduct an in-depth, rigorous audit of all modals present within the student-facing pages of my web application.

For every modal detected across the student pages, analyze and document it using the following structured breakdown:

1. Modal Identification & Purpose
   - Modal Name & Trigger: What component or student action opens it?
   - Page Location: Which student page or sub-view houses this modal?
   - Core Purpose: What specific student task, transaction, or notification does it serve?

2. UI (User Interface) Analysis
   - Visual Hierarchy: Headers, body layout, typography scale, icon usage, and contrast ratios.
   - Component & State Design: Form inputs, CTA buttons (primary, secondary, destructive), close triggers ('X' icon, backdrop click), and dynamic states (default, hover, active, disabled, loading, error).
   - Layout & Responsiveness: Modal sizing, padding, backdrop dimming, scrolling behavior (content scroll vs. full-page scroll), and mobile screen adaptation.

3. UX (User Experience) Analysis
   - Cognitive Load & Clarity: Is the copy clear, direct, and free of confusing jargon? Does it disrupt student focus appropriately?
   - Flow & Friction: How many steps are required to complete the action? Are validations inline or post-submission? Is there a risk of accidental data loss on close?
   - Accessibility (a11y): Keyboard navigation (focus trapping, Tab order, Esc-to-close), ARIA roles, and screen-reader friendliness.

4. Actionable Improvements
   - Critical Flaws: Bugs, design misalignments, or confusing patterns.
   - Recommended Redesign: Concrete, step-by-step suggestions to optimize visual appeal, student efficiency, and accessibility.

Present your findings modal-by-modal, followed by a prioritized summary table ranking all modals by UI/UX severity and polish.
